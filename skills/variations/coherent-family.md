# Coherent Variation Family Skill

Purpose:
Generate 10 coherent visual families rather than 40 unrelated designs.

For each family define:
- Family ID/name
- Visual concept
- Typography language
- Color treatment
- Motion language
- Transition language
- Audio language
- Brand intro treatment
- Main-topic intro treatment
- Sub-topic intro treatment
- Outro treatment

The four outputs in one family must feel related.

Do not randomly change the visual language between the four stages.
Across the 10 families, vary the creative direction meaningfully while preserving Tinitiate identity.
